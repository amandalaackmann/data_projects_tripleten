# Sprint 1 – Manhattan Airbnb Analysis

## 📌 Objective
Analyze Manhattan Airbnb listings to determine the most attractive neighborhoods, most popular property sizes, and top revenue-generating listings to guide investment and pricing strategies.

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Used
- Excel (pivot tables, formulas, conditional formatting)  
- Data cleaning and preprocessing  
- Data summarization and reporting  

## 📊 Key Steps
1. **Data import and inspection** – Loaded raw listings and calendar datasets into Excel.  
2. **Cleaning and formatting** – Removed nulls, standardized formats, verified numeric and date fields.  
3. **Analysis and metrics** – Created pivot tables to rank neighborhoods, property sizes, and annual revenues.  
4. **Visualization** – Used pivot tables to highlight trends in property popularity and revenue potential.  
5. **Summary of findings** – Drafted an executive summary highlighting main insights.  

## 📈 Results / Insights
- Most attractive neighborhoods had higher occupancy and revenue potential.  
- Most popular property size was one-bedroom units.  
- Top revenue listing generated an estimated **$359,280 annually**.  
- Premium locations outperformed others in revenue consistency.
