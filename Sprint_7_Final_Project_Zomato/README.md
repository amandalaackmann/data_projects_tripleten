# Sprint 7 – Final Project: Zomato Performance Analysis

## 📌 Objective
Evaluate sales performance across restaurants and cuisines to identify top revenue drivers, underperforming areas, and provide actionable recommendations to improve overall revenue.

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Used
- Excel (data cleaning, pivot calculations)
- Tableau (interactive dashboards, storytelling)

## 📊 Key Steps
1. **Data preparation (Excel)** – Cleaned the provided Zomato dataset and standardized fields needed for analysis.  
2. **Sales metrics** – Calculated total revenue, total orders, average order value, and summarized results by restaurant and cuisine.  
3. **Visualization (Tableau)** – Built dashboards with KPIs, Top Restaurants/Cuisines analysis, and a City Revenue Map.  
4. **Recommendations** – Suggested strategies based on findings, focusing on partnerships, cuisines, ratings, and metro cities.  

## 📈 Results / Insights
- **Top performers:** Domino’s Pizza and other leading chains generated the highest revenue.  
- **Top cuisines:** Pizza, Bakery Desserts, and American Snacks drove the strongest results.  
- **Geographic concentration:** Revenue is heavily concentrated in major metro areas such as Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore.  
- **Key recommendations:**  
  - Strengthen top restaurant partnerships  
  - Promote leading cuisines  
  - Support high-rated vendors  
  - Encourage menu focus  
  - Invest in metro city markets
